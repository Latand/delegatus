## As built

The build stage implemented the plan below with these concrete choices. Where a row of the table says otherwise, this section is what the code does.

**One question store (seat fence, 2026-10-11).** Operator decisions are asked through the prototype-review question rounds, the form lane 414b6c9a draws in the «Рішення» column (`docs/design/operator-decision-forms.md` §4). This lane keeps to automatic resolution, stale clean-up with a reason and Undo, and the seat as the first recipient of worker questions. It stores no question of its own: an escalation holds the decision round's id, and a stage asks in its `stage_report` summary or in a round it publishes. The plan's structured `decision` payload and `attention_action` tool below were not built.

- **Routing is a projection stamp.** `src/lib/attention/routing.ts` decides who answers each conversation reason and lane wait; `src/lib/attention/routeOverlay.ts` reads the seat record, the project's tick setting and the stored escalations, and `/api/files` stamps `attentionRoute` on the entries (`src/app/api/files/response.ts`), with the board's waiting decision rounds (`waitingRoundIds`) so an escalated wait follows its form. Every operator surface reads the stamp through the existing reason model (`reasonAsksOperator`, `laneNeed`, `pipelineAsks`), so the header, both panels, cards, rail and the `dismiss_attention` read count the same slice. The projection cache key includes the seat record and the tick settings (`operatorProjection.ts`).
- **What goes to the seat.** A delegated conversation's prose ask, native question or plan, memory kill and tool permission request (registry delegation depth or a pipeline/flow membership), every parked lane (`needs_decision`), a spent review budget on a lane an agent created, and a completed lane whose merge stopped. A project with no active seat, or whose tick is switched off, routes nothing: the operator answers. A lane waiting for an automatic provider retry is owned by `system` and asks nobody.
- **What stays the operator's.** A seat's own questions; direct operator sessions; owed deliveries; failed launches; the unreviewed head of a lane the operator created by hand; prototype choices and update decisions.
- **Seat wake.** A new wake reason `worker-ask` (`src/lib/monitor/types.ts`) carries one line per routed wait: who asked, the question in their words (a native question with its numbered options), the tool that answers it where it asked, and how to escalate it through a decision form. It is due on the next enabled check and shown once per wait and seat (`agendaVersion`), so a rotation re-offers it to the successor. The gather reads the same board projection (`src/lib/attention/seatAgenda.ts`).
- **Answering.** The seat answers prose asks with `send_message` to the asking conversation, native questions and plans with `conversation_action answer` (the question's reason id as `requestId`, then `answers` as option numbers per question, `text`, or `approve`), permissions with `conversation_action permission`, memory kills with `conversation_action`, lane decisions with `pipeline_action resolve-decision` and accepted heads with `accept-head`. `answer` reads the conversation's pending question from its transcript and refuses unless it is still the one named, builds the resolution the conversation's own question card sends (`src/lib/conversation/nativeAnswer.ts`), and submits it as the structured host's durable `answer` operation to the host's one open question (`src/lib/runtime/structuredControls.ts`); a terminal-hosted question keeps `dialog-key`. `mayAnswerPipelineDecision` (`src/lib/pipelines/engine.ts`) now lets the project's current designated seat answer a decision on any lane of its project and accept the head of a lane an agent created; moving a running stage keeps its old rule. `continue-review` stays refused; the stale wake hint that offered it now offers `accept-head` or escalation.
- **Escalation.** The seat asks the operator with `publish_prototype_review` (questions in their language, on a task of the project), then calls `dismiss_attention` with `escalate: {reviewId}` and a conversation target with its `reasonId`, or a pipeline target, under the dismissal's own authority gate. The round must exist in the target's project and still wait as the board draws it, with the dismissal overlay applied (`ROUND_NOT_FOUND`, `ROUND_NOT_WAITING`). The escalation is keyed by the exact wait (`reason:<id>` or the lane's kind and movement), so a newer ask or a lane that moved is routed afresh; `undo:true` withdraws it. While the round waits, the wait is stamped `owner: "form"`, whether or not the seat can be woken then: it draws no row, the form's row is the operator's one row for it, and it leaves the seat's agenda. Once the operator decides the round, dismisses it or a newer round replaces it, the wait is the seat's again, stamped with the round it came back from (`returnedFrom`), and that changes its agenda key, so the seat is woken once for the returned wait; unchanged polls stay quiet.
- **Automatic clearing.** `src/lib/attention/reconcile.ts` proves four rules: `retired-seat` (a seat replaced and its conversation ended: every reason it raised), `operator-replied` (an operator message admitted after a prose ask), `resumed` (a turn after a memory kill) and `stage-settled` (the stage attempt that asked settled after the reason was raised; a reason raised after the settlement, in a resumed conversation, is newer and stays). `src/lib/attention/reconcileController.ts` runs them every minute in the release that owns traffic, over the same board projection, and writes through the dismissal store with `by: {kind: "system", rule}` and a snapshot of what it hid (reason kind, raise time, row title, project). The cleared list of the needs-you read draws those records whether or not the source still raises the reason, and a card keeps the cleared reason with Undo for a day after the clear. Undo of such a record keeps it as `restoredAt`, so the same rule cannot clear the same reason again; a reason raised later can be cleared on new evidence.
- **Storage.** Escalations and restored markers live in the existing `attention_dismissals` collection as their own record kinds, which the projection key, the rollback mirror and retention already cover. No new collection was needed.
- **Row text.** `src/components/attention/operatorAsk.ts` gives every remaining row its question, options and one sentence (en and uk in `src/lib/i18n`). A lane row shows the stage's reported `needs_decision` summary as its question, else the mechanical stop's detail; a native question shows its own options. A question with choices is a decision round, whose own row carries it. The desktop panel and the phone sheet render it whole (`NeedsYouAskText.tsx`), with no clamp.
- **Counts.** Routed and cleared waits contribute zero to the operator's count. The board report's section 9 names how many waits wait on the seat first. No second panel or resolved-work feed was added; PR #2744's «Готово» section and its counter rules are untouched by this change.

## Why the panel accumulates work

1. **Evidence is advisory.** `needsYouEvidence()` marks `operator-wrote`, `ended`, `later-turn`, `task-done`, `lane-moved`, forge closure and newer work as stale (`needsYouRead.ts:70`). `needsYouAnswer()` counts those rows unchanged (`:172`). `boardReport.ts:476`, `:546` tells the seat to read and clear them later. No system reconciliation runs on those facts.
2. **Waiting is conflated with ownership.** `attention.ts:283` accepts a pending question regardless of parent/seat; `pipelineBlockModel.ts:26` maps both parked states to needs-you. `attentionQueue.ts:67` joins them before an owner is chosen. The worker can correctly wait for another agent and still increase the operator counter.
3. **Wake coverage misses the actual ask.** Seat reasons include lane events, terminal children, settled own lanes and permissions (`monitor/types.ts:185`). `seatTickSources.ts:1368` gathers infrastructure signals, and `seatTick.ts:1341` checks permission requests. A nonterminal worker's final prose question or a held plan has no dedicated ask item carrying its body/options and return address.
4. **Blocked merges repeat technical work on an operator surface.** The merge runner appropriately stops on red checks/conflicts (`autoMerge.ts:335`), but `needReason.ts:97` converts every blocked merge into an operator need. Routing cannot be implemented by weakening those checks.
5. **Labels omit the actionable payload.** `decision.ts:134` returns kind plus stage; `needsYouPanel.ts:56`, `:157` reuse that label. The actual question already survives in `attempt.report.summary`/`attempt.output` (`engine.ts:4389`), yet the panel never reads it. Desktop clamps the decision to two lines (`AttentionPanel.tsx:307`); phone truncates it (`MobileAttentionSheet.tsx:353`, `:435`).
6. **History depends on a reason still existing.** The cleared read reconstructs reasons from current files/lanes (`needsYouRead.ts:135`). When a source truly ends or disappears, its cleared reason disappears too. Automatic clearing needs a bounded saved display record so its reason and Undo remain available.
7. **Generic staleness is too broad for mutation.** Task completion, unrelated operator traffic, a closed PR and an ended worker can coexist with an unresolved prototype or lane question. Applying `facts.some(stale)` as a deletion policy would lose real choices. The action rule must prove that this exact obligation is settled or obsolete.

## Implementation plan for acceptance 2: exact cleanup and Undo

Add one small attention module, proposed `src/lib/attention/reconcile.ts`, with a pure `classifyAttention(candidate, facts)` interface. It returns the exact source identity, owner (`system`, `seat`, `operator`), resolution evidence when conclusive, the actual ask/options, and the existing target/action needed to answer it. Collect raw reasons without the operator visibility filter; classification must never call `readNeedsYou()` recursively. There is no model call in a projection or tick precheck.

**Conclusive rules:** an exact native request's accepted result/cancellation; a confirmed answer linked to the prose ask; a later turn/message that demonstrably supersedes that prose ask; an older retired-seat ask with ended generation and no live continuation; a report already answered/resolved/retired; an exact retry/relaunch/recovery success; a merged authoritative PR satisfying that lane's obsolete merge obligation; a later saved prototype decision superseding that exact round. Reconcile an ended stage conversation with its settled lane attempt first: a `needs_decision` verdict transfers the live obligation to the lane and must survive conversation cleanup.

Use `needsYouEvidence()` (`needsYouRead.ts:62`) as fact collection, and add exact request/attempt/continuation evidence. Its public `stale` field remains diagnostic during rollout; automatic resolution receives a separate affirmative rule code. Missing/unavailable sources produce no cleanup. Pass the observation's clock through all report TTL/evidence reads rather than the separate `new Date()` at `:89`.

**Hints requiring inspection:** `proc: done/killed` alone on a resumable direct session, an unrelated later admission, a later seat status report, another task assignment, task completion, a closed unmerged PR, a publisher lane moving past a latest undecided round, elapsed time and an unreadable source. A pending native question requires its tool/request identity to settle; arbitrary later chat traffic does not answer it. A replacement lane requires an explicit source/task supersession link before retiring an old decision.

Run reconciliation in the Viewer-owned raw files/board projection after all source overlays (`src/app/api/files/response.ts:851`, `:865`), before publishing the cached representation; also during `gatherSeatTickInput()` (`seatTickSources.ts:2355`). Both call the same collector/classifier and revalidate mutations. This makes cleanup happen on a normal poll or seat check without depending on an agent turn. The needs-you route's existing authority guard stays (`src/app/api/attention/needs-you/route.ts:13`). Reconciliation starts only after the existing release fence admits the Viewer; builds, MCP-only reads and agents cannot become live-state owners.

Write through the existing dismissal mechanisms (`dismissals.ts:445`, `:551`, `:556`), adding server-only `DismissedBy.kind: "system"` (`dismissalTypes.ts:14`, `:84`) and localized attribution in `decision.ts:181`. The write keeps a short redacted reason, a stable rule/evidence fingerprint, and an exact source revision. Do not use timestamp-only conversation clears; specify `reasonId`. Recheck request identity and lane movement under the relevant mutation lock; changed evidence returns “changed” and is re-evaluated next time. A deterministic operation key makes duplicate reconciliation a no-op.

Keep a minimal `attention_routes` SQLite collection, proposed `src/lib/attention/routes.ts`, keyed by exact source/reason or lane stage+attempt+movement (merge obligations also include blocked head/time). It holds only escalation, answer receipt, automatic-clear display snapshot and Undo override metadata. Current seat ownership is derived on every read, so rotation does not require rewriting each row. Open obligations are not aged away. Settled display snapshots use the existing 30-day/2,000-item bounded retention; do not evict unresolved deliveries to make room. Reuse `SqliteStateCollection`, existing authority and delivery stores; introduce no queue daemon or generic workflow framework.

Extend `clearedRows()` (`needsYouRead.ts:135`) to include saved automatically cleared rows after their source stops emitting a reason, with rule, reason, attribution and working Undo target. Show these in a collapsible cleared list inside the existing desktop panel/phone sheet. Scope and count remain decisions-only; the list itself contributes zero. Keep newest-first order and existing MCP byte bounds/pagination.

Undo removes the dismissal and records “operator restored this reason on evidence fingerprint E”. The same rule on E cannot clear it again. Undo restores attention for reconsideration; it never undoes a delivered answer, native permission result, saved prototype choice, completed lane or deployment. Explain that distinction on a restored settled item and offer the existing reopen/follow-up action where one is valid.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

- **Chosen: one server classification with existing dismissal, wake and answer controls.** It adds a small durable row for escalation/receipt/Undo state and pays for the required rotation, retry and polling behavior once. Source-specific checks keep valid choices visible.
- **Rejected: hide stale/worker rows only in the browser.** MCP, cards, notifications and seat tick would disagree; hidden asks would have no answer path and no durable reason/Undo.
- **Rejected: make the seat periodically clear all waits.** This is today's incomplete mechanism, dependent on an agent turn and vulnerable to ambiguous evidence.
- **Rejected: automatically choose every recommendation or accept every red review.** A recommendation proves no authority. Existing mandate, fixed budgets, explicit approval reservations and protected controls determine who can act.
- **Rejected: a new repair workflow engine or generic incident queue.** The lane, merge runner, seat tick and runtime journal already own the work and its delivery.

These are reversible additions to projections and local attention state. No hard-to-reverse architecture choice requires a separate ADR.
