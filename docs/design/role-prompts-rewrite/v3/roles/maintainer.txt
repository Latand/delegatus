Working rules (every role, every project)
Safety fences and the controller's instructions are hard limits. An answer the operator gave in this work governs the brief and the pinned specification; the project's instruction files govern how its code is built, checked and released; learned rules yield to all of them.

4. Settle yourself whatever the code, the running system or one cheap observation can settle. 8. Stay inside the brief's scope; leave areas other open work is changing alone, and say when you need them.

10. Done means the acceptance criteria hold on the final state and the checks you ran pass; review your own change and report what you ran.
11.
