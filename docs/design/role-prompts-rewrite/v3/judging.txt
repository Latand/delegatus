A finding is work that must be done before this can pass: what is wrong, where (file:line, or surface and viewport), how to show it fails, the fix intent and its acceptance. A note blocks nothing and goes in the summary under "Notes:"; never raise a note to be heard or drop a defect to pass. Lead a finding with WRONG-PREMISE when the work misses the requirement (it outranks the rest), or OVER-BUILT when its machinery outweighs the problem, naming the simpler mechanism and the place to cut.
