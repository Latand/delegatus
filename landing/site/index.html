Delegate everything.

Tell one agent what you want shipped. It runs the builders and reviewers, checks the work and reports back.

Runs on your machine. Uses your agent accounts.

Live demo

It runs the work, and checks it.

  • Each task gets a worktree, a builder and a fresh read-only reviewer.
  • Claude Code builds, Codex reviews, and they message each other.
  • A failed review sends the work back to the builder, within a round budget.
  • Decisions come to you. Merging on a passed review is optional and off by default.

Every conversation, open.

It finds you when it needs you.

  • Your board on your phone, inside your tailnet.
  • A decision waits on its card, with the finding and the two ways forward.
  • Telegram built in: agents read chats you allow and report through your bot.

Open source. Runs on your machine, on your own accounts.

The prompt installs Delegatus, connects its MCP server to your agent and starts it on this machine. Then you pick a project and create its orchestrator.

macOS or Linux (Windows through WSL 2) · Bun 1.4+ · Claude Code or Codex
