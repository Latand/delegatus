Delegatus: delegatus-gate-RUN_ID_1.service stops every agent after one OOM kill. Add this drop-in, then daemon-reload and restart the service when agents finish:
# $HOME/.config/systemd/user/delegatus-gate-RUN_ID_1.service.d/oom.conf
[Service]
OOMPolicy=continue
OOMScoreAdjust=100

systemctl --user daemon-reload
systemctl --user restart 'delegatus-gate-RUN_ID_1.service'
